Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Lantern Path mobile application (the "App") available on the Google Play Store.

Information Collection and Use

1. Personal Information

The App does not require any personal information for signup, and we do not collect any personal data from users. No email addresses, names, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information is requested or stored.

2. Usage Data

The App does not collect any usage data or track how you interact with the game. This includes no tracking of:

  • Which puzzles you solve or how long they take
  • Which scenes you visit
  • Your progress through the game
  • Clicks, taps, or any other interactions

3. Device Information

The App does not collect any device information, including:

  • Device model or manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, or advertising ID)
  • IP addresses
Note on Google Play: While our App collects no data directly, Google Play's standard distribution platform may collect anonymized, aggregate installation and crash data for app improvement purposes as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions

The App requests no special permissions on your device. It does not require access to:

  • Internet (fully offline game)
  • Storage / Photos / Media
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Location
  • Contacts

Data Storage

All game data (such as saved progress via SharedPreferences) is stored locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since the App collects no data at all, no special considerations are needed.

Third-Party Services

The App uses no third-party services that collect data, including:

  • No analytics (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.)
  • No advertising networks
  • No crash reporting services
  • No social media integrations

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
